📰 Real-World Pipeline Scenario: “Munich Olympic Bid”

1. EXTRACTED ARGUMENTS (A)

A1 (Economy): Reusing existing venues revitalizes sports infrastructure and local economy.
A2 (Fiscal Risk): Cost overruns impose unpredictable burdens on taxpayers.
A3 (Safeguard): Substantial reserve buffers and strict transparency rules mitigate fiscal risks.

2. ARGUMENT GRAPH & ATTACKS (R)

A2 attacks A1 | A3 defends A1 against A2 | A2 ↔ A3 form a dilemma axis

3. COMPUTED PERSPECTIVES

Perspective 1 (Pro-Bid): {A1, A3}
AI Thesis: Sustainable opportunity for regional sports and economy under controlled fiscal risk.
Perspective 2 (Anti-Bid): {A2}
AI Thesis: Uncontrollable expenditure risks outweigh any potential long-term benefits.
